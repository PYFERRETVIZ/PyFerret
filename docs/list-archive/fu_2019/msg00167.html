Hi Karen,

These sets of symbols such as Cartographic, Math, etc, are not available for PLOT/VS but are only for use in labels. That's the case for both PyFerret and classic Ferret. I think your best option would be to look at the PyFerret namedsymbols , and perhaps experiment with defining your own custom symbol, which is described just below that section. You make a little file with the shape defined, and then call that file by naming it.

I've added a couple of notes to ../../documentation/users-guide/ferret-fonts/list-of-ferret-fonts to clarify this.

Ansley
