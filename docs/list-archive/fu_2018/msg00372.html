Olivier,
Here is a simple workaround if it is only a question of the y-axis label. Just replace your last command with the following 4 lines: 
 
shade/nolab/set t2m[y=-2:2@ave,l=1:5000@sbx:250]
ppl ylab "Years before Present"
ppl xlab "Longitude"
ppl shade
 
James

The following script gives more details : 
 
USE"https://vesg.ipsl.upmc.fr/thredds/dodsC/store/p86mart/IPSLCM5A2/PROD/Holocene/TR5AS-Vlr01/ATM/Analyse/TS_YE/TR

! Model Years are from 2000 to 7999, which correspond to nothing :-(

! But the labelling I want is Year before present (present being 1850)

!DEFINE AXIS/T/UNITS="Years"/NAME="YearBP" YearBP

SHADE  t2m[Y=-2:2@AVE,L=1:5000@SBX:250]
